The Margin Leak Finder

Paste a sales export into a Google Sheet. Find out which of your products still make money after true costs — materials, labor minutes, packaging, shipping, card fees charged the way processors actually charge them, refunds, and overhead. Free. No email. Your data never leaves your spreadsheet.

What's in it

  • engine.gs + main.gs — the tool (paste into Apps Script; setup steps in the README)
  • checker.py — an independent Python re-implementation, so you can verify the tool's numbers
  • engine_v1.gs — the first build, kept so you can see what was wrong with it
  • sales_export.csv + product_costs.csv — the fictional sample business (Copperline Candle Co.)
  • CLAUDE_PROMPT.txt — all three prompts, word for word

What it found on the sample business

  • The best seller by units was losing $1.88 on every sale — $4,435.90 a year of leaks at run rate
  • A gift set that looked like 14.4% EARNING was LEAKING once refunds were priced in
  • The report reconciles to the export to the cent, and refuses to grade anything while revenue is unaccounted
